# Photos

Drop photos here and they replace the matching stand-in automatically.
Use JPG, PNG or WebP, at least 1600 px wide for `hero` and 1000 px for the rest.

| File name (any of .jpg .jpeg .png .webp) | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| `hero` | Big photo at the top of the home page |
| `gifting` | Gift banner on the home page |
| `story` | "Our story" section on the home page |
| `category-barfi`, `category-ladoo`, `category-halwa`, `category-milk-sweets`, `category-namkeen` | Category cards |
| `sweet-kaju-katli`, `sweet-gulab-jamun`, … | Each sweet (name in lower case, spaces → dashes) |

The images here now are 3D renders of a Mithai Palace gift box made with
`blender/make_boxes.py` (see `blender/README.md`). They are illustrations, not
photos of the shop's own products. Overwrite any of them with a real photo of the
same name when you have one.
